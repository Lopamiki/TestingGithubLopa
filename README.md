# Student Activities & Registration System

A web-based platform for students to explore and register for various campus activities.

## 🎯 Features

- **Browse Activities**: View all available campus activities with descriptions and schedules
- **Register/Unregister**: Easily sign up or withdraw from activities
- **View Capacity**: See current enrollment vs. maximum capacity for each activity
- **Student Dashboard**: Track all your registered activities in one place
- **Local Storage**: All registrations are automatically saved in your browser

## 📚 Available Activities

1. **Debate Club** - Enhance your public speaking skills
2. **Basketball** - Team sports and fitness
3. **Photography** - Capture moments and learn composition
4. **Coding Bootcamp** - Advanced programming skills
5. **Music Ensemble** - Perform and collaborate with musicians
6. **Environmental Club** - Make a difference for our planet

## 🛠️ How to Use

1. Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. Enter your name in the input field
3. Browse the available activities
4. Click "Register" to join an activity
5. View your registrations in the "Your Activities" section
6. Your data is automatically saved!
